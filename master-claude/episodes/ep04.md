## Script — English (spoken)

```
Stop! Do you explain the same thing to Claude in every new chat? Every time you type: I'm learning a new language, I'm a beginner... and you start from zero. The fix: Projects. One: Create a project, a space with its own chats. Two: Add your files to its knowledge base. Three: Write project instructions once. See the difference... every new chat starts with your context. And Projects are available even on the free plan. Save this video and follow for a new skill every day.
```
